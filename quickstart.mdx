This guide walks you through everything you need to do to go from zero to active on Orpheus. By the end, you'll have a complete account, a configured profile, and a clear picture of what to do next — whether you're posting a job, applying to one, or managing candidates for a client.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request Early Access">
    Orpheus is in pre-launch, so access is invitation-based for now. Head to [orpheus.thaloz.com](https://orpheus.thaloz.com) and join the mailing list. You'll receive an email with your invitation link as soon as access opens for your region or role type.

    If your agency or team has an urgent need to get started sooner, reach out directly at [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com) — the Thaloz team reviews early access requests on an ongoing basis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Once you receive your invitation, open the link and sign up with your work email address. Choose a strong, unique password for your account.

    After submitting the sign-up form, check your inbox for a confirmation email and click the verification link inside. Your account won't be active until your email is confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Role">
    When you first log in, Orpheus asks you to select your role. Choose the one that best describes how you'll use the platform:

    - **Candidate** — You're looking for job opportunities and want to be discovered by employers and recruiters.
    - **Employer / Client** — You represent a company that's hiring and you want to post jobs and manage applicants.
    - **Recruiter** — You're a staffing professional who sources candidates, manages client relationships, and drives placements.

    Your role determines what features and views you see inside Orpheus. If you're unsure which to choose, see [User Roles](/concepts/user-roles) for a detailed breakdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Your Profile">
    A complete profile helps Orpheus connect you with the right opportunities, candidates, or clients. Fill in all the relevant fields for your role:

    - **Candidates** — Add your full name, a professional summary, your skills, experience level, preferred work arrangement, and your resume or portfolio.
    - **Employers** — Add your company name, a description of your organization, your industry, and the types of roles you typically hire for.
    - **Recruiters** — Add your professional background, the industries you specialize in, and information about the clients or candidate pools you work with.

    You can update your profile at any time, but starting with complete information means you'll get better results from day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take Your First Action">
    With your profile set up, you're ready to put Orpheus to work. What you do next depends on your role:

    - **Candidates** — Browse open roles that match your skills and submit your first application.
    - **Employers** — Create your first job posting and start receiving applications from qualified candidates.
    - **Recruiters** — Search the candidate pool, review incoming applications for your active roles, or start a conversation with a promising candidate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Have questions at any point during setup? Email the Thaloz team at [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com). They're a hands-on group and respond quickly, especially for teams onboarding during the early access period.
</Tip>
