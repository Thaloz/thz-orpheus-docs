At Thaloz, protecting your personal information is a core commitment. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Orpheus platform collects from you, how that data is used, when it may be shared with others, and what rights you have over your information. Whether you use Orpheus as a Candidate, Employer, or Recruiter, this policy applies to you.

**Last Updated:** April 30, 2026

<Note>
  By creating an account or continuing to use the Orpheus platform, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Information We Collect">
    **Information You Provide**

    When you register and use Orpheus, you may provide us with:

    - **Identity & contact details** — your name, email address, phone number, and postal address
    - **Professional information** — your resume, work history, education, skills, and certifications
    - **Credentials** — professional licenses, qualifications, or other verifiable credentials
    - **Payment information** — billing details required to process subscription or service fees

    **Information Collected Automatically**

    When you access the Platform, we automatically collect certain technical and usage data, including:

    - **Device & network data** — IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
    - **Usage data** — pages visited, features used, search queries, and interaction patterns within the Platform
    - **Cookies and similar technologies** — see the Cookies section below for details
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How We Use Your Information">
    Thaloz uses the information we collect to:

    - **Provide and maintain the Platform** — operate all core features including job matching, application management, and messaging
    - **Improve our services** — analyze usage patterns to develop new features and enhance the user experience
    - **Communicate with you** — send important account updates, support responses, and, where you opt in, marketing communications
    - **Ensure security** — detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
    - **Meet legal obligations** — comply with applicable laws, regulations, and lawful requests from authorities
    - **Personalize your experience** — tailor job recommendations, search results, and platform content to your profile and preferences
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Google Workspace API Data">
    Orpheus integrates with Google Workspace APIs to support calendar and scheduling features within the Platform.

    <Warning>
      Data obtained through Google Workspace APIs is used **exclusively** to power user-facing features — specifically, calendar and interview scheduling functionality. This data is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the feature you are using.
    </Warning>

    Thaloz's use of Google Workspace API data adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the **Limited Use** requirements. We do not share, sell, or transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide the scheduling features you have requested, or as required by law.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How We Share Your Information">
    Thaloz does not sell your personal information. We share your data only in the following circumstances:

    - **With your consent** — when you explicitly authorize us to share your information with a specific party
    - **Service providers** — with trusted third-party vendors who help us operate the Platform (e.g., hosting, payment processing, analytics), under strict data protection agreements
    - **Business transfers** — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, where your data may transfer to the acquiring entity
    - **Legal requirements** — when required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or to protect the rights and safety of Thaloz, our users, or the public
    - **Job matching** — as a core function of the Platform, Candidate profiles are shared with relevant Employers, and Employer job postings are shared with relevant Candidates, to facilitate recruitment
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Data Security">
    Thaloz takes the security of your personal information seriously and implements multiple layers of protection:

    - **Encryption in transit** — all data transmitted between your browser or app and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols
    - **Encryption at rest** — stored data is encrypted to protect it against unauthorized access at the infrastructure level
    - **Regular security assessments** — we conduct ongoing security reviews and vulnerability assessments to identify and address risks
    - **Access controls** — access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel who need it to perform their job functions, and is governed by role-based access policies

    While we apply strong security measures, no system is completely immune to risk. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your account credentials confidential.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your Privacy Rights">
    Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

    - **Access** — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
    - **Correction** — ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete data
    - **Deletion** — request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
    - **Opt-out** — withdraw consent for marketing communications or certain data processing activities at any time
    - **Portability** — receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
    - **Restriction** — ask us to restrict how we process your data in certain circumstances

    To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com). We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cookies and Tracking Technologies">
    Orpheus uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate and improve the Platform. The types of cookies we use include:

    - **Essential cookies** — required for the Platform to function correctly, such as keeping you logged in and enabling core features. These cannot be disabled without affecting Platform functionality.
    - **Analytics cookies** — help us understand how users interact with the Platform so we can improve performance and usability.
    - **Preference cookies** — remember your settings and preferences (such as language or display options) to personalize your experience.
    - **Marketing cookies** — used to deliver relevant advertisements and track the effectiveness of promotional campaigns.

    You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or our in-platform cookie consent manager. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some Platform features.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Children's Privacy">
    The Orpheus platform is not intended for use by individuals under the age of **16**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe that a child under 16 has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us at [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com) and we will promptly delete that information.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="International Data Transfers">
    Thaloz is headquartered in Uruguay, and your data may be processed and stored there or in other countries where our service providers operate. When we transfer personal data across borders, we take appropriate safeguards to ensure that your information remains protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws — including the use of standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions, or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="California Privacy Rights (CCPA)">
    If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you additional rights regarding your personal information:

    - **Right to Know** — you may request details about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, as well as how it is used and shared.
    - **Right to Delete** — you may request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
    - **Right to Opt-Out** — you have the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. Thaloz does not sell personal information.
    - **Right to Non-Discrimination** — you will not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your CCPA rights.

    To submit a CCPA request, contact us at [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="European Privacy Rights (GDPR)">
    If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) provides you with the following rights:

    - **Lawful basis for processing** — we process your data on the basis of contract performance, legitimate interests, legal obligations, or your consent, depending on the type of processing.
    - **Access and portability** — you can request a copy of your data in a portable format.
    - **Rectification** — you can ask us to correct inaccurate data.
    - **Erasure ("right to be forgotten")** — you may request deletion of your data where there is no compelling reason for us to continue processing it.
    - **Restriction and objection** — you may ask us to restrict processing or object to certain uses of your data.
    - **Withdrawal of consent** — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
    - **Right to lodge a complaint** — you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

    To exercise your GDPR rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Changes to This Policy">
    We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our data practices, services, or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and notify you via email or an in-platform notice where required.

    We encourage you to review this policy regularly. Your continued use of the Platform after any changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
  </Accordion>
