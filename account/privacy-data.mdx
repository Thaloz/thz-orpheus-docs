Orpheus collects and uses your personal and professional information to operate the platform, match candidates with opportunities, and support recruiters and employers in managing the hiring process. Thaloz takes your privacy seriously — your data is protected with industry-standard security measures, and you have meaningful rights over how it's used.

## Data Orpheus collects

When you use Orpheus, the platform collects several categories of information depending on your account type and how you use the service.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Personal Information" icon="address-card">
    Name, email address, phone number, and postal address.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Professional Information" icon="briefcase">
    Resume, work history, education, skills, certifications, and professional credentials.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Account & Activity Data" icon="chart-bar">
    Login credentials, account preferences, and your activity on the platform.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payment Information" icon="credit-card">
    Billing address and payment method details, processed securely by third-party payment processors.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Orpheus collects only the information necessary to provide its services. You control much of what you share by choosing what to include in your profile.

## How your data is used

Orpheus uses your data to:

- **Provide the platform** — create and manage your account, connect candidates with recruiters and employers, and operate all platform features.
- **Match candidates to jobs** — use professional profile data to surface relevant opportunities and qualified candidates.
- **Improve services** — analyze how the platform is used to identify improvements, fix issues, and develop new features.
- **Communicate with you** — send account notifications, scheduling updates, billing information, and — where you've opted in — relevant product updates.
- **Process payments** — handle billing and subscription management through secure third-party payment processors.

Orpheus does not sell your personal data to third parties.

## Your privacy rights

You have the following rights over your personal data in Orpheus. To exercise any of them, contact [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com).

  <Card title="Correction" icon="pen-to-square">

Thaloz will respond to privacy rights requests within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

## Data security

Thaloz protects your data using industry-standard security practices:

- **Encryption in transit** — all data transmitted between your browser or app and Orpheus servers is encrypted using TLS.
- **Encryption at rest** — your stored data is encrypted on Orpheus servers to protect it from unauthorized access.
- **Regular security assessments** — Thaloz conducts ongoing security reviews to identify and address potential vulnerabilities.

If you believe your account has been compromised or you've identified a potential security issue, contact [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com) immediately.

## Exercising your rights

To make a privacy request — whether to access, correct, delete, export, or opt out of the use of your data — contact the Thaloz team:

**Email:** [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com)

Include your account email address and a clear description of your request. Thaloz may ask you to verify your identity before processing the request to protect your account security.
