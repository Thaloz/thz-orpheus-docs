Orpheus offers subscription-based access to platform features for candidates, employers, and recruiters. Understanding how billing works — including renewal cycles, payment methods, and placement fees — helps you stay in control of your account and avoid unexpected charges.

## Subscription plans

Orpheus provides subscription services that give you access to the platform's core features based on your account type. Subscriptions are billed on a recurring basis — monthly or annually depending on the plan you select — and renew automatically at the end of each billing period.

<Note>
  Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period. To avoid being charged for the next cycle, cancel your subscription before your renewal date. See [Managing your subscription](#managing-your-subscription) for cancellation steps.
</Note>

## Managing your subscription

### Viewing your current plan

To see your active plan, billing cycle, and next renewal date:

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Your current plan, billing frequency, and renewal date appear at the top of the page.

### Upgrading or changing your plan

To switch to a different plan:

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Click **Change Plan**.
3. Select the plan you want and confirm the change. If you upgrade mid-cycle, any prorated difference is applied to your next invoice.

### Canceling your subscription

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you retain full access to the platform until then.

To cancel:

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**.
3. Follow the confirmation steps to finalize the cancellation.

You'll receive an email confirmation once your cancellation is processed. If you change your mind before the billing period ends, you can reactivate your subscription from the same page.

## Payment methods

Accurate billing information is required to maintain uninterrupted access to Orpheus. To add or update your payment details:

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Under **Payment Method**, click **Update Payment Method**.
3. Enter your new billing address and payment information.
4. Click **Save**.

Update your payment details promptly if your card expires, your billing address changes, or you switch payment accounts. Failed payments may result in interrupted access to platform features.

<Info>
  Orpheus processes payment details securely through third-party payment processors. Your full card number and sensitive payment data are handled directly by these processors and are not stored on Orpheus servers.
</Info>

## Placement fees

For employers and recruiters, successful candidate placements may be subject to placement fees in addition to subscription charges. Placement fee structures vary and may include:

- **Percentage of annual salary** — a fee calculated as a percentage of the placed candidate's first-year compensation
- **Fixed fee** — a flat amount agreed upon in advance
- **Other arrangements** — custom terms as defined in your service agreement with Thaloz

The specific placement fee structure, amounts, and payment terms applicable to your account are set out in your separate service agreement. Review your agreement or contact [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com) if you have questions about placement fee terms.

## Refunds

Orpheus does not offer refunds except as required by applicable law or at Thaloz's sole discretion. If you believe a charge was made in error, contact [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com) within a reasonable time of the charge date with your account details and a description of the issue.
