Your Orpheus account settings give you full control over how your profile appears, how the platform communicates with you, and which external services are connected. Keeping your account information accurate helps ensure you receive the right opportunities, communications, and platform access for your role.

## Profile information

Your profile is how Orpheus identifies you and how other users — recruiters, employers, or candidates — see your information. To update your profile:

1. Go to **Settings > Profile**.
2. Edit any of the following fields: **Full Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, or **Postal Address**.
3. Click **Save Changes** to apply your updates.

Your email address is used for login and platform notifications, so make sure it stays current if your contact details change.

## Account credentials

To change your password:

1. Go to **Settings > Security**.
2. Click **Change Password**.
3. Enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password.
4. Click **Update Password**.

Choose a strong, unique password for your Orpheus account. Do not share your credentials with anyone. If you believe your account has been compromised, change your password immediately and contact [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com).

<Note>
  You are responsible for all activity that occurs under your account. Keep your login credentials secure and log out of shared or public devices after each session.
</Note>

## Notification preferences

Orpheus can notify you by email and through in-platform alerts about activity relevant to your account — such as new messages, application status changes, scheduled interviews, or billing updates.

To manage your notifications:

1. Go to **Settings > Notifications**.
2. Toggle individual notification types on or off according to your preference.
3. Click **Save Preferences**.

You can turn off marketing communications at any time without affecting essential account notifications such as security alerts or billing receipts.

## Integrations

Connect external services to extend what Orpheus can do. The **Integrations** settings page shows all available connections and their current status.

Currently available integration:

- **Google Workspace** — connect your Google account to enable calendar scheduling and interview coordination. See [Connect Google Workspace to Orpheus](/integrations/google-workspace) for setup instructions.

<Warning>
  Keeping your account information accurate is a platform requirement. Outdated or incorrect profile details — especially your email address and payment information — may affect your access to platform features, communications, and billing.
</Warning>

## Account termination

If you want to close your Orpheus account, you can do so through your account settings or by contacting the Thaloz team directly:

- **Through the platform:** Go to **Settings > Account** and select **Close Account**. Follow the confirmation steps to permanently deactivate your account.
- **By email:** Send a request to [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com) with your account email address. The Thaloz team will process your request and confirm when your account has been closed.

Account closure is permanent. Any active subscriptions should be canceled before closing your account to avoid additional charges. See [Billing](/account/billing) for subscription cancellation steps.
