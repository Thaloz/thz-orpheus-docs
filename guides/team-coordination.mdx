High-volume staffing depends on tight coordination across multiple people and roles. Orpheus brings recruiters, employers, and candidates into a shared operating environment where everyone works from the same information, pipeline view, and communication history. When your team is aligned in one system, placements move faster and nothing falls through the cracks.

## How team coordination works in Orpheus

Orpheus is built as a multi-role platform. Recruiters, employers, and candidates each operate from their own dashboard and permissions, but their activity connects in real time. When a recruiter advances a candidate, the employer sees it. When an employer updates a job listing, the recruiter's pipeline reflects the change immediately.

This shared-state model means your team doesn't need to synchronize through email or external spreadsheets. The platform maintains a single source of truth for every placement in progress — from the moment a job is posted through the final offer.

## Recruiter workflows

Recruiters in Orpheus manage activity across multiple client accounts and candidate pipelines simultaneously. From the recruiter dashboard, you can:

**Manage candidate pipelines for clients** — View and advance candidates across every active listing you're supporting. Filter by client, job, experience level, or pipeline stage to focus on what needs attention most.

**Coordinate between candidates and employers** — Act as the communication bridge when direct messaging between a candidate and employer runs through your workflow. Review messages in context, add your own notes, and ensure both parties stay informed.

**Track placements and follow-ups** — Monitor the status of every active placement you're managing, from initial screening through offer acceptance. Use the follow-up tools to flag candidates or listings that need a timely response before they go stale.

<Info>
  Recruiters on Orpheus have a professional responsibility to act in the best interests of both candidates and clients — not just one side of a placement. This means giving candidates honest guidance about fit, keeping clients informed about realistic timelines, and never steering either party toward outcomes that serve your metrics at their expense. Orpheus is designed to support placements that work long-term.
</Info>

## Working with clients

Employers (clients) and recruiters collaborate directly within Orpheus rather than through back-and-forth outside the platform. As a recruiter, you can:

- **Access client listings** — View and manage job listings on behalf of clients you're authorized to support.
- **Share candidate profiles** — Surface shortlisted candidates to clients directly through the platform, with full profile context attached.
- **Align on pipeline decisions** — Use shared pipeline views so clients can see candidate status and provide input without requiring separate status update calls or emails.
- **Coordinate on offer stages** — Work with clients on the offer and close phase, keeping the process moving and candidates engaged.

Keeping clients informed at each stage — even when there's nothing new to report — builds trust and reduces friction in the working relationship.

## Communication tools

Orpheus includes built-in messaging across every part of the platform. You can message candidates directly from their application, reach out to clients about a listing, or communicate within a team context. Every message is tied to the relevant job or candidate record, so conversations are always in context.

Key communication features available to recruiters:

- **Application-level messaging** — Message candidates directly within their application thread.
- **Client messaging** — Communicate with employers about specific listings or candidates from within the relevant record.
- **Notification system** — Receive alerts when candidates update their status, employers respond to applications, or pipeline stages change.

All communication through Orpheus is logged and retained, creating a clear record that protects both recruiters and the people they work with.

## Best practices

Effective team coordination on Orpheus comes down to consistent habits. Follow these practices to keep your operation running smoothly:

**Keep candidate statuses up to date** — A pipeline is only useful if it reflects reality. Update candidate stages as soon as decisions are made, and mark positions as closed the moment they're filled. Stale data creates confusion and delays for everyone involved.

**Use messaging for all placement-related communication** — Keeping conversations inside Orpheus means nothing gets lost, and both candidates and clients can trust they have the full picture. Avoid taking placement discussions off-platform where they can't be tracked or referenced later.

**Maintain professional standards** — Every interaction you have on behalf of your agency reflects on Orpheus, your clients, and your candidates. Respond promptly, communicate honestly about timelines and fit, and treat all parties with the same level of professionalism you'd want for yourself.

**Assign clear ownership** — When multiple recruiters work on the same account or listing, establish who owns each candidate or pipeline stage. Clear ownership prevents duplicated outreach, candidate confusion, and internal miscommunication.

**Review your pipeline daily** — A daily scan of your active placements helps you catch anything that's stalled, flag candidates who need follow-up, and identify listings that may need additional sourcing attention before they fall behind.
