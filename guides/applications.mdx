Orpheus centralizes the entire application process for candidates, employers, and recruiters in one place. Candidates can apply, track their status, and communicate directly with hiring teams. Employers and recruiters can review incoming applications, move candidates through pipeline stages, and keep communication organized — all without leaving the platform.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="For Candidates">
    ## Applying for a job

    When you find a role you want to pursue, the application process is straightforward:

    1. Open the job listing and read the full description, required skills, work type, and location details carefully.
    2. Confirm the role matches your experience level and preferences before proceeding.
    3. Click **Apply** on the listing page. Orpheus automatically attaches your candidate profile to the application.
    4. Add any optional notes or a cover message if the listing invites one, then submit.

    Your application is immediately visible to the employer or recruiter managing the role.

    <Note>
      Apply only for positions that genuinely match your qualifications and experience level. Submitting applications to roles you're not qualified for reduces your visibility to recruiters and employers over time, and takes attention away from candidates who are a strong fit. Quality applications lead to better outcomes than volume alone.
    </Note>

    ## Application statuses

    After you apply, Orpheus tracks your application through several stages. You can see your current status for every active application in the **Applications** section of your dashboard.

    | Status | What it means |
    |---|---|
    | **Submitted** | Your application has been received and is awaiting review. |
    | **Under Review** | The employer or recruiter is actively reviewing your profile and application. |
    | **Shortlisted** | You've been selected for further consideration — expect follow-up communication. |
    | **Interview Scheduled** | A meeting or interview has been arranged; check your messages for details. |
    | **Offer Extended** | The employer has made you an offer — review the details in your messages. |
    | **Closed** | The position has been filled or the application is no longer active. |

    ## Following up via messaging

    If your application has been under review for a while and you haven't heard back, you can send a professional follow-up through Orpheus messaging. Open the application, navigate to the **Messages** tab, and send a brief, courteous note expressing your continued interest.

    Keep follow-ups concise and professional — one message is appropriate. Orpheus messaging keeps all communication in context, so both parties have a clear record of the conversation.

    ## Withdrawing an application

    If your situation changes and you're no longer interested in a role, you can withdraw your application at any time:

    1. Go to **Applications** in your dashboard and open the relevant application.
    2. Click **Withdraw Application** and confirm your decision.

    Withdrawing an application removes you from the candidate pipeline for that role. The employer is notified so they can move forward without waiting on your response.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="For Employers & Recruiters">
    ## Viewing incoming applications

    All applications submitted for your job listings appear in the **Applications** section of your dashboard. You can view them in aggregate across all active listings or filter by a specific job.

    To access applications for a specific listing:

    1. Go to **Jobs** in your dashboard and open the relevant listing.
    2. Select the **Applicants** tab to see everyone who has applied.
    3. Sort or filter by application date, status, or experience level to prioritize your review.

    ## Reviewing candidate profiles

    Click any applicant's name to open their full candidate profile alongside their application. You'll see their professional summary, work history, education, skills and certifications, and work preferences — everything you need to make an informed decision about next steps.

    Take the time to review the full profile rather than relying on the summary alone. Candidates who have invested in a complete profile are often stronger, more engaged applicants.

    ## Moving candidates through stages

    Orpheus uses a pipeline model to track where each candidate stands in your hiring process. From the applicant view, you can advance or update a candidate's status at any time:

    - **Shortlist** candidates you want to prioritize for further review.
    - **Schedule an interview** by advancing the candidate to the Interview Scheduled stage and coordinating through messaging or your connected calendar.
    - **Extend an offer** by moving the candidate to the Offer Extended stage.
    - **Close** an application if the candidate is no longer being considered.

    Keeping statuses current ensures your pipeline accurately reflects reality and prevents candidates from being left in limbo.

    ## Using messaging to communicate

    Every application in Orpheus includes a built-in messaging thread. Use it to communicate directly with candidates — requesting additional information, scheduling next steps, or providing status updates. Keeping all communication within Orpheus creates a clear record and ensures nothing gets lost across different channels.

    <Tip>
      Use messaging proactively. Candidates who receive timely updates — even brief ones acknowledging receipt or confirming next steps — report a significantly better experience and are more likely to remain engaged throughout the process. Professional, responsive communication reflects well on your organization.
    </Tip>

    ## Closing a position

    Once you've filled a role or decided not to proceed with a hire:

    1. Open the job listing and click **Close Position**.
    2. Orpheus will prompt you to update the status of any remaining open applications before archiving the listing.
    3. Confirm the closure to archive the listing and notify candidates whose applications are still active.

    Closed listings and their associated application history are retained in your dashboard for reporting and reference purposes.
  </Tab>
