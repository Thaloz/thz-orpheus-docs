Orpheus gives employers a streamlined way to post and manage job listings that reach the right candidates quickly. Whether you're filling a single role or running high-volume hiring campaigns, the jobs dashboard keeps every listing organized and actionable from posting through placement.

## Creating a job listing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Jobs section">
    From your employer dashboard, select **Jobs** in the main navigation. This is your central hub for all active, paused, and closed listings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new listing">
    Click **Post a Job** in the top-right corner of the Jobs page. A new listing form opens, ready for you to fill in the details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the job details">
    Complete each field in the listing form:

    - **Job title** — Use a clear, standard title candidates will search for.
    - **Job description** — Describe responsibilities, expectations, and what makes this role compelling.
    - **Required skills** — Add the specific skills candidates must bring to the role.
    - **Experience level** — Choose from Junior, Mid, Senior, Lead, or Architect.
    - **Work type** — Select Full Time, Part Time, Contract, Temporary, or Freelance.
    - **Location type** — Choose Remote, Hybrid, or In Person.
    - **Urgency level** — Set Low, Medium, High, or Urgent to signal hiring timeline to candidates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    Preview your listing to confirm all details are accurate and complete. When you're satisfied, click **Publish** to make the listing visible to candidates on Orpheus.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Job listing fields

Every published listing displays the following information to candidates browsing opportunities:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Job title** | The primary label candidates see in search results and listings. |
| **Job description** | Full details about the role, including day-to-day responsibilities and team context. |
| **Required skills** | A structured list of skills used to match candidates to the role. |
| **Experience level** | Signals the seniority expected — Junior, Mid, Senior, Lead, or Architect. |
| **Work type** | Clarifies the employment arrangement: Full Time, Part Time, Contract, Temporary, or Freelance. |
| **Location type** | Indicates whether the role is Remote, Hybrid, or In Person. |
| **Urgency level** | Communicates how quickly you need to fill the role — from Low to Urgent. |

<Tip>
  Setting a higher urgency level — **High** or **Urgent** — surfaces your listing more prominently to active candidates and signals that you're ready to move quickly through the hiring process. Use it when you have a genuine near-term deadline.
</Tip>

## Managing active listings

Once a job is published, you can update or change its status at any time from the Jobs dashboard.

**Editing a listing** — Click the listing title to open it, then select **Edit**. You can update any field and republish without losing existing applicants.

**Pausing a listing** — If you need to temporarily stop accepting new applications without closing the role, click **Pause**. The listing becomes invisible to candidates but remains accessible in your dashboard.

**Closing a listing** — Once a position is filled or cancelled, click **Close**. Closed listings are archived and no longer visible to candidates. You can reopen a closed listing later if needed.

## Viewing applicants

To review candidates who have applied to a specific role:

1. Open the **Jobs** section and click the relevant listing.
2. Select the **Applicants** tab within the listing view.
3. Browse candidate profiles, review submitted materials, and move applicants through pipeline stages.

You can also access all incoming applications across every listing from the **Applications** section in your dashboard, filtered by job.

<Warning>
  You are responsible for ensuring every listing you post on Orpheus meets the following standards:

  - **Post only legitimate job openings** — listings must represent real, available roles.
  - **Provide accurate descriptions** — job details, requirements, and compensation information must be truthful and current.
  - **Comply with employment laws** — listings must not discriminate on the basis of protected characteristics and must align with applicable labor regulations.
  - **Use candidate data only for recruitment** — information submitted by applicants may not be used for any purpose outside evaluating candidates for the posted role.

  Listings that violate these standards may be removed and your account may be suspended.
</Warning>
