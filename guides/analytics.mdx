Understanding how your recruitment operation is performing is essential to improving it. Orpheus Analytics gives authorized users — employers and recruiters — a clear view of pipeline health, candidate sourcing effectiveness, team activity, and placement speed. Rather than relying on intuition or manual tracking, you can act on accurate, up-to-date data drawn directly from your activity on the platform.

<Note>
  Analytics and reporting features are available to authorized users only — specifically employers and recruiters. Candidates do not have access to the Analytics section.
</Note>

## What analytics covers

Orpheus Analytics surfaces data across the core dimensions of your recruitment operation:

**Recruitment pipeline metrics** — See how candidates are distributed across each stage of your pipeline, where volume is concentrated, and where candidates are dropping off or stalling. This gives you a real-time picture of pipeline health across all active listings.

**Candidate sourcing data** — Understand where your candidates are coming from and which sourcing approaches are generating the strongest applicant pools. Use this to focus effort on the channels and strategies that deliver results.

**Time-to-fill** — Track how long it takes to move from job posting to accepted placement for each role. Broken down by listing, experience level, work type, or recruiter, time-to-fill data reveals where your process is efficient and where delays are accumulating.

**Team performance** — Monitor recruiter activity, pipeline ownership, and placement outcomes across your team. Identify high performers, spot workload imbalances, and ensure accountability across the operation.

<Info>
  Thaloz continuously optimizes Orpheus based on aggregated platform-wide data, helping surface best practices and improve the tools available to your team. The insights in your Analytics dashboard reflect your organization's specific activity — use them in combination with your team's judgment to drive continuous improvement in your own operation.
</Info>

## Accessing analytics

To open Analytics:

1. Log in to your Orpheus dashboard as an employer or authorized recruiter.
2. Select **Analytics** from the main navigation menu.
3. Use the filters at the top of the Analytics page to scope the data by date range, listing, recruiter, or job type.

The default view shows an overview of your operation's current state. Navigate the tabs within Analytics to drill into specific metrics — pipeline, sourcing, time-to-fill, or team activity.

## Key metrics to track

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Active Positions and Fill Rate" icon="briefcase">
    Monitor how many roles are currently open and what percentage of recently closed positions were successfully filled. A low fill rate signals a sourcing, screening, or process problem worth investigating.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Candidate Pipeline Stages" icon="chart-bar">
    See candidate volume at each stage — submitted, under review, shortlisted, interview, offer. Lopsided distributions often reveal a bottleneck: too many candidates stalling at one stage before moving forward.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Time from Posting to Placement" icon="clock">
    Track the average number of days between when a job goes live and when an offer is accepted. Segment this by experience level, work type, or recruiter to identify which variables most affect your speed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team Activity" icon="users">
    Review application review rates, stage advance frequency, and message response times across your recruiter team. Consistent team activity correlates strongly with faster placements and better candidate experience.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Using data to improve

Analytics is most valuable when it drives action. Use these approaches to turn your data into meaningful operational improvements:

**Identify and address pipeline bottlenecks** — If a large proportion of candidates are stalling at the same stage across multiple listings, investigate why. Is there a screening question that's eliminating too many candidates? Are interview scheduling delays slowing things down? Isolate the stage, diagnose the cause, and adjust your process.

**Adjust sourcing based on what's working** — If your sourcing data shows that a particular approach is generating candidates who advance further in the pipeline, invest more in that channel. Redirect effort away from sources that produce volume but low-quality matches.

**Set time-to-fill benchmarks** — Use historical data to establish realistic targets for different role types and experience levels. Measure against those benchmarks regularly and investigate outliers — roles taking significantly longer than the benchmark are worth a closer look.

**Review team performance regularly** — Share relevant metrics with your recruiter team on a regular cadence. Transparency about performance creates accountability and gives your team the information they need to self-correct before small gaps become larger problems.

**Connect analytics to business outcomes** — Where possible, link placement data back to downstream outcomes like retention and client satisfaction. Placements that close quickly aren't valuable if they don't stick. Use Orpheus Analytics as one input in a broader picture of recruitment quality.
