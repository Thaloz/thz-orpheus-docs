Your candidate profile is how Orpheus connects you with the right opportunities. A well-built profile lets recruiters and employers understand your background at a glance, and it powers the matching logic that surfaces relevant job listings for you. Investing a few minutes in completing every section significantly improves the quality of opportunities you receive.

## Your candidate profile

Your profile serves as your professional presence on Orpheus. It brings together your work history, skills, certifications, and preferences into a single place that travels with every application you submit. Recruiters and employers reviewing your applications see your full profile alongside your application materials, giving them the context they need to evaluate you quickly and accurately.

A complete profile also helps Orpheus surface opportunities that match your actual experience and preferences, rather than presenting every open role indiscriminately.

## Profile sections

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Personal Information">
    Your personal information section includes your name, email address, and phone number. Keep these details current so recruiters and employers can reach you through Orpheus messaging or directly when appropriate. This section is visible to employers and recruiters when you apply to or express interest in a role. You can also upload your resume here — it will be attached automatically to every application you submit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Professional Summary">
    Your professional summary is a short narrative — typically two to four sentences — that describes who you are professionally, what you specialize in, and what you're looking for next. Write it in first person and keep it focused on what makes you a strong candidate for the types of roles you're targeting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Work History">
    List your previous roles in reverse chronological order. For each position, include the employer name, your job title, the dates you held the role, and a brief description of your responsibilities and accomplishments. Quantify impact where you can — numbers and outcomes make your experience more compelling to reviewers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Education">
    Add your educational background, including degrees, diplomas, and any relevant coursework or academic achievements. For each entry, include the institution name, qualification earned, and the year you completed it. This section supports roles where specific educational credentials are required or preferred.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Skills and Certifications">
    List your professional skills and any certifications you hold. Skills are used directly in candidate matching, so include both hard skills (tools, technologies, methodologies) and soft skills relevant to your field. For certifications, include the issuing organization and, where applicable, the expiration or renewal date to keep your profile accurate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Work Preferences">
    Your work preferences help Orpheus and recruiters understand what you're looking for in your next role. Specify your experience level (Junior, Mid, Senior, Lead, or Architect), preferred work type (Full Time, Part Time, Contract, Temporary, or Freelance), and location preference (Remote, Hybrid, or In Person). Setting clear preferences means you'll receive opportunities that actually fit your situation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Complete every section of your profile — even sections that feel optional. Orpheus uses your full profile to match you with relevant opportunities. Candidates with complete profiles, including skills, certifications, and work preferences, appear in significantly more recruiter searches than those with partial profiles.
</Tip>

## Keeping your profile current

Your profile reflects where you are in your career right now. Update it whenever your situation changes:

- **After completing a role** — Add the position to your work history while the details are fresh.
- **After earning a certification** — Add it to your skills and certifications section immediately so it factors into matching.
- **When your preferences change** — If you've shifted from seeking full-time roles to contract work, or from in-person to remote, update your work preferences so Orpheus doesn't continue routing you toward opportunities that no longer fit.
- **Before applying for a specific role** — Review your profile to make sure it reflects the skills and experience most relevant to that opportunity.

Staying active on Orpheus — logging in regularly and keeping your profile updated — also signals to recruiters that you're engaged and responsive.

## Profile visibility

Your profile is not publicly searchable on Orpheus. It is shared with employers and recruiters in two situations:

1. **When you apply for a job** — Submitting an application automatically shares your full profile with the employer and any assigned recruiter.
2. **When you express interest in a role** — If you indicate interest in a listing, the relevant recruiter or employer gains visibility into your profile.

Outside of these actions, your profile remains private. You control when and with whom it is shared by choosing which roles you apply to or express interest in.

<Note>
  When you apply for a role or express interest in a listing, your profile information — including your work history, skills, and contact details — is shared with the employer and the recruiter managing that placement. This sharing is strictly for recruitment purposes. For full details on how your data is handled, review the [Privacy and Data settings](/account/privacy-data) in your account.
</Note>
