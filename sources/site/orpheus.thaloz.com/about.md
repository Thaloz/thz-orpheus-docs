# About Orpheus by Thaloz

Your signal in the noise of tech recruiting.

## Who We Are

Focused on assembling teams with Latin American talent, Thaloz has expanded its scope to deliver a range of solutions—providing top nearshore talent to multiple clients, including Fortune 500 companies.

Orpheus is our flagship recruitment platform, designed to streamline the process of connecting exceptional tech professionals with innovative companies. We leverage cutting-edge technology to make talent discovery and hiring more efficient, transparent, and human-centric.

## Our Mission

We're not your typical software company; we stand out as a **people-focused consulting partner**. With 100+ experts and a global presence spanning 30 countries, we prioritize both our dedicated support team and our community of experts.

## Our Values

### Remote-First

We believe you should be free to live and work anywhere you like. Our culture is fully remote, with our team distributed all over the world.

### Data-Driven

We are a vibrant, data-driven company with a young spirit. We make decisions based on insights and continuously optimize our processes.

### Community Focused

We are deeply committed to fostering a positive impact within the tech community. We share experiences, inspire others, and nurture meaningful exchange.

### Thaloz House

Our headquarters in Montevideo serves as a vibrant space for collaboration and dialogue, embodying our commitment to in-person connection.

## What We Offer

Partner with us for strategic consulting, access to top-tier talent, and seamless implementation of complex technologies.

- **Strategic Consulting:** Expert guidance for your digital transformation journey
- **Top-Tier Talent:** Access to vetted Latin American tech professionals
- **Complex Technology Implementation:** End-to-end support for your most challenging projects

## Our Story

Thaloz was **founded in 2020** and is based in **Montevideo, Uruguay**. From our humble beginnings, we've grown into a global team of 100+ experts operating across 30 countries.

Our journey has been driven by a simple belief: that the best talent shouldn't be limited by geography, and that companies deserve access to exceptional professionals regardless of where they're located.

"We're building the future of work—one connection at a time."
