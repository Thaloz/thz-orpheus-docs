Your Orpheus account is the foundation for everything you do on the platform — from applying to jobs to managing a full recruiting pipeline. This page walks you through creating your account, choosing the right account type for your role, completing your profile with the information that matters most, and keeping your account secure over time.

## Creating Your Account

Orpheus is currently in pre-launch. To create an account, you first need an invitation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Early Access List">
    Visit [orpheus.thaloz.com](https://orpheus.thaloz.com) and sign up for the mailing list. You'll receive an invitation email when access opens. If you need access sooner, contact [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com) to request early entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Your Invitation">
    When your invitation arrives, click the link inside. It takes you directly to the sign-up form with your email address pre-filled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Password">
    Choose a strong password — at least 12 characters, mixing letters, numbers, and symbols. You'll use this password every time you log in to Orpheus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Your Email">
    After submitting the sign-up form, Orpheus sends a confirmation email to your address. Click the verification link to activate your account. Check your spam folder if you don't see it within a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing Your Account Type

When you first log in, Orpheus asks you to choose your account type. This determines which features you can access and how the platform presents information to you. Select the type that matches how you'll use Orpheus.

| Account Type | Best For | What You Can Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Candidate** | Professionals looking for new opportunities | Build a talent profile, browse and apply to open jobs, message recruiters and employers, track your application status |
| **Employer / Client** | Companies with open roles to fill | Post job listings, review and manage applicants, communicate with candidates, track hiring pipelines, access analytics |
| **Recruiter** | Staffing professionals managing placements | Source and manage candidates, oversee multiple client relationships, coordinate team workflows, run analytics across all active roles |

<Note>
  Choose your account type carefully — it shapes your entire experience inside Orpheus. If you need to change your account type after setup, contact [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com) and the support team will help you make the switch.
</Note>

## Completing Your Profile

A complete, accurate profile helps Orpheus match you with the right opportunities, candidates, or clients. The information you need to provide varies by account type.

### For Candidates

Your profile is your professional presence on Orpheus. Employers and recruiters will use it to evaluate whether you're a fit before they reach out.

- **Full name** — Use the name you go by professionally.
- **Email address** — Keep this current; it's how Orpheus and other users will contact you.
- **Resume or portfolio** — Upload a current resume in PDF format and, if relevant, link to a portfolio or GitHub profile.
- **Skills** — Add specific technical and professional skills. Be precise — this is what recruiters search for.
- **Experience level** — Indicate whether you're early-career, mid-level, senior, or a specialist. This helps surface the right roles.
- **Work preferences** — Specify your preferred work arrangement (remote, hybrid, on-site), availability, and any location or timezone requirements.
- **Professional summary** — Write a short paragraph describing your background, what you do best, and what kind of opportunity you're looking for.

### For Employers

Your company profile tells candidates and recruiters who you are and what it's like to work with you.

- **Company name and logo** — Use your official company name and upload a clear logo.
- **Company description** — Explain what your company does, your industry, and what makes your organization a good place to work.
- **Company size and location** — Let candidates know how large your team is and where you're based.
- **Job descriptions** — When you post roles, include detailed descriptions with responsibilities, requirements, and compensation range where possible.
- **Billing information** — If your plan requires it, add billing details in your account settings before posting jobs.

### For Recruiters

Your profile establishes your credibility with both candidates and clients on the platform.

- **Full name and professional title** — Be specific about your role and specialty.
- **Professional background** — Describe your recruiting experience, the industries you work in, and the types of roles you focus on.
- **Client relationships** — Link or associate your profile with the employer accounts you represent so candidates can see who you're sourcing for.
- **Contact preferences** — Set how and when you prefer to receive messages from candidates.

## Account Security

Keeping your account secure protects your data, your candidates' information, and your clients' trust.

- **Use a strong, unique password** — Don't reuse your Orpheus password on other services. If you think your password has been compromised, change it immediately in your [account settings](/account/settings).
- **Keep your contact information current** — An up-to-date email address ensures you can always recover your account and receive important platform notifications.
- **Log out on shared devices** — If you access Orpheus on a shared or public computer, always log out when you're done.
- **Review your account activity** — Periodically check your account for anything that looks unfamiliar. If you notice unexpected activity, contact [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com) right away.

<Note>
  Orpheus requires that the information you provide in your profile be accurate and up to date, as outlined in the platform's [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). Misleading or false information — including fabricated credentials, company details, or job listings — may result in your account being suspended. When in doubt about what to include, reach out to [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com) for guidance.
</Note>
