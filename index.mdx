## What You Can Do with Orpheus

Orpheus is built around the three people who make every placement happen — candidates, employers, and recruiters. Here's what each of them can do on the platform.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Post and Manage Jobs" icon="briefcase">
    Employers create detailed job listings and track every applicant through a structured pipeline, from first contact to offer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Build a Standout Profile" icon="id-card">
    Candidates present their skills, experience, and work preferences so the right opportunities find them — not the other way around.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Coordinate Your Team" icon="users">
    Recruiters keep hiring workflows moving with shared candidate views, task tracking, and team-level visibility across all active roles.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Communicate in Context" icon="message">
    Built-in messaging keeps conversations tied to the right job or candidate, so nothing important gets buried in someone's inbox.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Track Performance with Analytics" icon="chart-bar">
    Monitor pipeline health, time-to-fill, and team activity with dashboards designed for high-volume recruiting operations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connect Google Workspace" icon="google">
    Sync calendars, documents, and contacts with your existing Google Workspace setup so Orpheus fits naturally into how you already work.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Get Up and Running

You can be active on Orpheus in just a few steps. Here's the path from sign-up to your first meaningful action on the platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Early Access List">
    Orpheus is currently in pre-launch. Visit [orpheus.thaloz.com](https://orpheus.thaloz.com) and add your name to the mailing list to be among the first teams on the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Once access opens, sign up with your email and choose a secure password. You'll confirm your email before your account becomes active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Role">
    Select whether you're joining as a **Candidate**, an **Employer/Client**, or a **Recruiter**. Your role shapes what you see and what you can do inside Orpheus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Your Profile">
    Fill in your profile with accurate, up-to-date information. Candidates add skills and experience; employers add company details; recruiters add professional context and client relationships.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Working">
    Post your first job, apply to an open role, or start reviewing your candidate pipeline — you're ready to go.
  </Step>
</Steps>
