## What Orpheus Does

Orpheus covers the full operational surface of a recruiting agency — from the first job posting to the final placement. Here are the core capabilities built into the platform.

- **Job Posting** — Employers and recruiters can create detailed job listings with requirements, location preferences, and role-specific details. Published jobs are immediately visible to matching candidates.
- **Candidate Profiles** — Candidates build structured profiles with their skills, experience level, resume, and work preferences. Recruiters can search and filter across the full candidate pool.
- **Application Tracking** — Every application moves through a defined pipeline with clear status updates. You always know where each candidate stands and what needs to happen next.
- **Team Coordination** — Recruiters and hiring managers share a real-time view of active roles and candidate progress. Tasks, notes, and updates stay tied to the right record.
- **Messaging** — Built-in messaging keeps conversations in context. Whether you're communicating with a candidate, a client, or a teammate, the right conversation is always one click away.
- **Analytics** — Dashboards surface pipeline health, hiring velocity, and team performance metrics so you can spot bottlenecks and make better decisions, faster.
- **Google Workspace Integration** — Orpheus connects with Google Calendar, Google Drive, and your Google contacts so it fits into the tools your team already uses every day.
