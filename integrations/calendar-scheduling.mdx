With your Google Workspace integration active, Orpheus turns interview scheduling into a single workflow. You select time slots, add participants, and send invites — all from inside the platform, with everything synchronized to Google Calendar in real time.

## Requirements

Before you can schedule interviews through Orpheus, your Google Workspace account must be connected:

- Your Orpheus account must have an active Google Workspace integration.
- You must have granted calendar read and write permissions during setup.
- Participants added to an interview must have valid email addresses on their Orpheus profiles to receive invites.

<Info>
  If you haven't connected Google Workspace yet, visit [Connect Google Workspace to Orpheus](/integrations/google-workspace) to set up the integration before scheduling.
</Info>

## Scheduling an interview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate application">
    Navigate to the candidate application you want to schedule an interview for. You can find applications in your **Pipeline** or through the **Candidates** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Schedule Interview">
    On the application detail page, click **Schedule Interview** (or the equivalent scheduling button for your account type). This opens the scheduling panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select available time slots">
    Orpheus displays your connected calendar's availability. Choose one or more time slots that work for the interview. You can also propose multiple options for the candidate to choose from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add participants">
    Add the candidate and any hiring team members who should attend. Each participant receives a calendar invite automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the interview">
    Click **Confirm** to finalize the event. Google Calendar invites are sent immediately to all participants, and the event appears on the application timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing scheduled events

After an interview is scheduled, you can manage it directly from the candidate's application page:

- **View** — open the application timeline to see all scheduled and completed interviews, including date, time, participants, and status.
- **Reschedule** — click the event and select **Reschedule** to pick a new time slot. Updated invites are sent automatically to all participants.
- **Cancel** — select **Cancel Interview** to remove the event. Participants receive a cancellation notice via Google Calendar.

Any changes you make in Orpheus are reflected on the original Google Calendar event, keeping everyone's calendars up to date.

## Availability and time zones

Orpheus displays all calendar availability in the time zone set on your Orpheus account. When participants are in different time zones, the platform automatically converts times so each person sees the interview in their local time on their calendar invite.

If you work with candidates or hiring teams across multiple countries — Thaloz's platform supports hiring operations across more than 30 countries — double-check that each participant's time zone is correctly set on their Orpheus profile to ensure invites display the right local time.

<Tip>
  If you're running high-volume screening, set up recurring availability blocks on your Google Calendar for interview windows (for example, Tuesday and Thursday afternoons). Orpheus reads these as open slots, making it faster to schedule back-to-back interviews without manual coordination.
</Tip>
