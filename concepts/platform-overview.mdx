Orpheus is the operating backbone for high-volume staffing agencies, giving your team a single place to manage every stage of the recruitment lifecycle — from posting positions and sourcing candidates to coordinating interviews and closing placements. Whether you work as a recruiter, represent a client organization, or are searching for your next role, Orpheus keeps every part of the process connected and moving.

## The Orpheus Platform

Orpheus unifies the three pillars that drive effective recruitment: **recruiting operations**, **candidate flow**, and **team coordination**. Instead of juggling disconnected tools, your entire process lives in one focused system. Recruiters manage pipelines and client relationships without switching context. Employers post roles and track progress in real time. Candidates discover opportunities and move through a transparent application journey. Every action in Orpheus feeds the same shared workspace, so nothing falls through the cracks.

## How Orpheus Fits Into Your Workflow

A typical recruitment cycle in Orpheus follows a clear, end-to-end path. An employer or recruiter **posts a job**, defining the role requirements, work type, and urgency. A candidate **discovers and applies** to the position using Orpheus's filtered search to find opportunities that match their experience and preferences. The recruiter **reviews the application** against the candidate's profile and moves promising candidates forward in the pipeline. When it's time to connect, **interviews are scheduled** directly through the Google Workspace calendar integration, keeping coordination inside the platform. Once a decision is made, the recruiter **confirms the placement** and closes the role — with the full history captured in Orpheus for reporting and future reference.

## Platform Availability

Orpheus is a remote-first platform, fully accessible from any device with an internet connection. Your team members, clients, and candidates can log in and collaborate from anywhere in the world — no on-site setup or local installation required. This makes Orpheus particularly well suited for distributed staffing teams and global talent searches.
