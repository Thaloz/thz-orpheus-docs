Candidate flow is the end-to-end journey a candidate takes inside Orpheus, from the moment they first search for an opportunity to the point where a placement decision is made. Understanding this flow helps candidates navigate the process with confidence, and gives recruiters and employers a clear picture of how candidates move through the system. Every stage is tracked inside Orpheus so all parties stay informed without chasing updates.

## The Candidate Journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discovery">
    The journey begins when a candidate searches for open opportunities on the platform. Orpheus provides a set of focused filters that let you narrow results to roles that genuinely match your situation. You can filter by experience level, work type, location preference, and how urgently the employer needs to fill the role. The result is a shortlist of relevant positions rather than an overwhelming list of unfiltered postings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Application">
    Once you identify a role that fits, you submit your application directly through Orpheus. Your candidate profile — including your experience, skills, and preferences — forms the foundation of your application. You do not need to rebuild your information for each submission; your profile travels with you. Some roles may include additional questions or requirements that you complete at the time of applying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    After you apply, the recruiter and employer assigned to the role review your profile and application. They assess your background against the position requirements and decide whether to move you forward in the pipeline. You can track the status of your application from your candidate dashboard. Recruiters may reach out through the platform's messaging system if they have questions or want to discuss your background further.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interview Coordination">
    When a recruiter or employer decides to move forward with you, they initiate interview scheduling through the platform. Orpheus integrates with Google Workspace, allowing calendar invites and meeting coordination to happen directly inside the system. You receive scheduling details without needing to exchange personal contact information outside the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decision">
    Following the interview, the recruiter and employer reach a placement decision. If you are selected, the recruiter confirms the placement and closes the role in Orpheus. If the role is not the right fit, the recruiter may keep your profile active for future opportunities or follow up with feedback. Either way, your profile and application history remain available for subsequent opportunities in the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Opportunity Filters

Orpheus gives you precise control over which opportunities appear in your search results. Use the following filters — individually or in combination — to surface the roles most relevant to you.

**Experience Level**
Match your seniority to the role's expectations. Available options are:
- **Junior** — early-career roles with foundational requirements
- **Mid** — roles expecting solid independent experience
- **Senior** — roles requiring deep expertise and ownership
- **Lead** — positions involving team leadership and strategic input
- **Architect** — high-level roles focused on system design and organizational direction

**Work Type**
Filter by the employment structure that fits your situation:
- **Full Time** — standard ongoing employment
- **Part Time** — reduced hours engagements
- **Contract** — fixed-term project-based work
- **Temporary** — short-duration roles covering specific needs
- **Freelance** — independent, flexible engagements

**Location**
Specify where and how you want to work:
- **Remote** — fully location-independent roles
- **Hybrid** — a mix of remote and in-person work
- **In Person** — roles requiring on-site presence

**Urgency**
See how quickly an employer needs to fill a role:
- **Low** — the employer is hiring on a relaxed timeline
- **Medium** — a standard hiring pace with no immediate pressure
- **High** — the employer is actively prioritizing this placement
- **Urgent** — the role needs to be filled as quickly as possible

## What Happens to Your Application

Once you submit an application, it enters the recruiter's pipeline immediately. Recruiters and employers can view your full profile alongside your application, and they move candidates through defined stages — typically from applied, to under review, to interview, and finally to a placement decision. You can see your current stage at any time from your candidate dashboard.

From the employer's perspective, Orpheus surfaces all applications for a given role in one place, making it straightforward to compare candidates and take action. Recruiters coordinate between you and the employer, so you always have a point of contact if questions arise. The process is designed to be transparent: you are never left wondering whether your application was received, and status updates reflect real pipeline movement rather than automated non-responses.

<Tip>
  Keep your candidate profile up to date — including your current experience level, preferred work type, and location flexibility. Orpheus uses your profile data to improve opportunity matching, and a complete, accurate profile significantly increases your visibility to recruiters actively filling relevant roles.
</Tip>
