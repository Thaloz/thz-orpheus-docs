Orpheus is a multi-role platform, and the experience you have depends on the role you hold. Candidates search and apply for opportunities, employers post positions and evaluate talent, and recruiters facilitate the entire placement process. Understanding which role applies to you — and what it unlocks — helps you get the most out of the platform from day one.

## Roles at a Glance

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Candidate">
    ### What Is a Candidate?

    A Candidate is an individual using Orpheus to find and apply for employment opportunities. You may be actively looking for a new role or simply keeping your profile available for future opportunities.

    ### What You Can Do

    - Build and maintain a professional profile with your experience, skills, and preferences
    - Search open positions using filters for experience level, work type, location, and urgency
    - Submit applications directly to roles that match your background
    - Communicate with recruiters and employers through the platform's messaging system
    - Track the status of your applications through the candidate pipeline view
    - Coordinate interview scheduling through the Google Workspace calendar integration

    ### Key Responsibilities

    As a Candidate, you are responsible for keeping your profile accurate and up to date, responding promptly to recruiter and employer messages, and engaging professionally throughout the application process. The quality of your profile directly affects how well Orpheus can match you to relevant opportunities.

    ### Getting Started

    Set up your Candidate account and complete your profile to start applying. See [Account Setup](/account-setup) for step-by-step instructions.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Employer">
    ### What Is an Employer?

    An Employer (also referred to as a Client) is a company or organization using Orpheus to find and hire talent. You work with recruiters and review candidates to fill open positions within your organization.

    ### What You Can Do

    - Post job openings with detailed requirements, work type, location preferences, and urgency level
    - Browse the candidate pool and review profiles surfaced by recruiters
    - Track application progress through the pipeline for each of your open roles
    - Communicate with recruiters and candidates through the platform's messaging tools
    - Schedule and coordinate interviews using the Google Workspace calendar integration
    - Access analytics and reporting on your active roles and hiring activity

    ### Key Responsibilities

    As an Employer, you are responsible for providing accurate and complete job descriptions, reviewing candidate profiles in a timely manner, and maintaining clear communication with the recruiters managing your account. Your responsiveness directly affects how quickly Orpheus can move candidates through the pipeline on your behalf.

    ### Getting Started

    Set up your Employer account and post your first role to begin receiving candidates. See [Account Setup](/account-setup) for step-by-step instructions.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Recruiter">
    ### What Is a Recruiter?

    A Recruiter is a recruitment professional who uses Orpheus to manage candidate pipelines, coordinate with employer clients, and facilitate placements. Recruiters are the connective tissue of the platform, working across both the candidate and employer sides.

    ### What You Can Do

    - Post and manage job openings on behalf of employer clients
    - Source and review candidate profiles across the platform
    - Manage the full application pipeline from submission through to placement
    - Communicate with both candidates and employers through integrated messaging
    - Schedule interviews and coordinate meetings using the Google Workspace calendar integration
    - Generate analytics reports to track pipeline performance and team activity
    - Collaborate with other recruiters on shared roles and client accounts

    ### Key Responsibilities

    As a Recruiter, you are responsible for managing candidate and client relationships professionally, keeping pipeline stages current and accurate, and ensuring timely communication at every stage of the placement process. Your activity in Orpheus is visible to the clients you serve, so accuracy and responsiveness are essential.

    ### Getting Started

    Set up your Recruiter account and connect with your team to begin managing pipelines. See [Account Setup](/account-setup) for step-by-step instructions.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Permissions and Access

Each role in Orpheus comes with a distinct set of permissions that determines what you can see and do. Candidates access their own profile, applications, and relevant job listings. Employers access their posted roles, associated candidate pipelines, and messaging with their assigned recruiters. Recruiters have the broadest access, managing roles, candidates, and client accounts across the platform. These boundaries ensure that sensitive information — such as candidate contact details or employer hiring budgets — is only visible to the appropriate parties.

## Switching Roles

Orpheus accounts are tied to a specific role at registration. If you operate in more than one capacity — for example, if you are both a recruiter and occasionally a candidate — you will need a separate account for each role. This keeps permissions clean and ensures that your activity in each capacity remains distinct. If you need guidance on managing multiple accounts, contact [hello@thaloz.com](mailto:hello@thaloz.com).
